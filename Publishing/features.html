Mental Breaks

Lost a Passion

Pawns with many passions may suffer a mental break and lose interest in a skill.

A random passion is downgraded from major to minor or removed entirely.
  • Intensity: Extreme
  • Eligibility: More than 3 passions required
  • Immunity: Pawns with 3 or fewer passions are immune
  • Commonality: Scales with passion count / total skills
  • Effect: Random passion downgraded or removed
  • Memory: Grants Catharsis on recovery

Reconsider Behavior

Pawns with many traits may suffer a mental break that changes their personality.

Either removes a good or neutral trait or adds a new bad or neutral trait.
  • Intensity: Extreme
  • Eligibility: Has good/neutral traits to remove OR space for new traits
  • Removes: Good traits or neutral traits (with reduced weight)
  • Adds: Bad traits (mostly) or neutral traits
  • Max traits: 3 (configurable 1-6)
  • Memory: Grants Catharsis on recovery
