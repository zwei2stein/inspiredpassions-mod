[h1]Inspired Passions and Traits[/h1]

Your colony is prosperous, your pawns have all the comforts and support they could have, constantly inspired and striving. Yet they never pick up an interest in a new skill or become well-adjusted members of society by overcoming their jealousy and taking up jogging.

They are all living in hell, throwing tantrums, giving up, and walking in a daze. Yet pawns never just give up being kind or start viewing taking care of animals as a chore instead of a fun activity.

[h2]Features[/h2]

This mod adds two new inspirations: one that improves traits and one that gives passions. It also adds two mental breaks that do the exact opposite.

[list]
    [*]New inspiration: gain new passions - an inspiration that can occur for pawns with few passions. It is less likely to happen to pawns with many passions.
    [*]New inspiration: improve traits - an inspiration that can occur for pawns with negative traits or with few positive traits. It is less likely to happen to pawns with good traits.
    [*]New mental break: lose passion - a mental break that can occur for pawns with many passions. Pawns with few passions will not experience this break, and it is more likely to affect pawns with many passions.
    [*]New mental break: degrade traits - a mental break that either removes a positive trait or gives a pawn a negative trait. It is less likely to affect pawns with many negative traits and few positive traits.
[/list]

[h2]On passions[/h2]

Pawns with a trait that matches a skill they are not passionate about (like a brawler pawn not having a melee skill passion) are more likely (but not that much) to gain that passion when inspired.

[h2]On neutral traits[/h2]

When inspiration gives a trait or a mental break removes a trait, a pawn can also gain or lose a neutral trait. So even if a pawn is inspired, they might not necessarily get a good trait.

For example, the Recluse trait gives a mood boost or penalty depending on how one plays the game, so it is a neutral trait.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Mod compatibility[/h2]

If a mod adds new traits, they will not be affected unless patched. This mod will not add or remove them.

This is because the mod will not know which traits are positive, negative, or neutral. On top of that, some traits are special - for example, Anomaly Expansion's Creepjoiner traits or a pawn's sexual orientation - and should not be freely removed from pawns or given to them.

You can see how mods/traits are configured in mod settings with dev-mode enabled.
