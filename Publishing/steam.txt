[h1]Inspired Passions and Traits[/h1]

Your colony is prosperous, your pawns have all the comforts and support they could have, constantly inspired and striving. Yet they never pick up an interest in a new skill or become well-adjusted members of society by overcoming their jealousy and taking up jogging.

They are all living in hell, throwing tantrums, giving up, and walking in a daze. Yet pawns never just give up being kind or start viewing taking care of animals as a chore instead of a fun activity.

[h2]Features[/h2]

This mod adds two new inspirations: one that improves traits and one that gives passions. It also adds two mental breaks that do the exact opposite.

[list]
    [*]New inspiration: Find a New Passion - an inspiration that can occur for pawns with few passions. It is less likely to happen to pawns with many passions.
    [*]New inspiration: Reconsider Behavior - an inspiration that can occur for pawns with bad traits or with few good traits. It is less likely to happen to pawns with good traits.
    [*]New mental break: Lost a Passion - a mental break that can occur for pawns with many passions, and the more passions they have, the more likely it is. Pawns with 3 or fewer passions will not experience this break.
    [*]New mental break: Lost Themselves - a mental break that either removes a good trait or gives a pawn a bad trait. It is less likely to affect pawns with many bad traits and few good traits.
[/list]

[h2]On passions[/h2]

Pawns with a trait that matches a skill they are not passionate about (like a Brawler without a melee passion) are slightly more likely to gain that passion when inspired.

[h2]On traits[/h2]

Some traits are straightforward: always good or always bad. Some have pros and cons - they are neutral (see below). And some are best left untouched - they are Static.

Players can override this evaluation per trait in the mod settings.

[h2]On neutral traits[/h2]

When an inspiration or a mental break gives or removes a trait, it can also be a neutral trait. So even if a pawn is inspired, they might not necessarily get a good trait.

For example, the Recluse trait gives a mood boost or penalty depending on how one plays the game, so it is a neutral trait.

The weight of neutral traits can be changed in the settings.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Mod compatibility[/h2]

Traits added by other mods are Unknown and will not be affected unless patched or explicitly set by the player as Good, Neutral, or Bad. Otherwise, this mod will not add or remove them.

This is because the mod will not know which traits are good, bad, or neutral. On top of that, some traits are special - for example, the Anomaly expansion's creepjoiner traits or a pawn's sexual orientation - and should not be freely removed from pawns or given to them, so they are Static.

You can see and edit how traits are configured in the mod settings (Per-Trait Settings tab).
